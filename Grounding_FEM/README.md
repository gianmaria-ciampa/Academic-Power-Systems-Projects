FEM Analysis of Grounding Electrodes in Homogeneous and Layered Soils
This project analyzes different grounding electrode configurations using Finite Element Method (FEM) simulations. The study evaluates electrical behavior, potential distribution, and step voltage in homogeneous and non‑homogeneous soils.

1. Spherical Electrode in Homogeneous Soil
1.1 Data & Properties
Definition of geometric parameters, soil resistivity, boundary conditions, and electrode characteristics.

1.2 Post‑Processing
Extraction of electric field, potential distribution, and current density around the electrode.

1.3 Graphical Results
Visualization of potential profiles and field intensity.
Step Voltage Analysis  
Evaluation of surface potential differences around the electrode.

2. Hemispherical Electrode in Homogeneous Soil
2.1 Data & Properties
Model setup for a hemispherical electrode buried in uniform soil.

2.2 Post‑Processing
Computation of grounding resistance and potential distribution.

2.3 Graphical Results
Plots of electric field and potential gradients.
Step Voltage Analysis

3. Hemispherical Electrode in Layered Soil (Double Layer)
3.1 Data & Properties
Definition of two‑layer soil model with different resistivities.

3.2 Post‑Processing
Evaluation of how soil stratification affects grounding performance.

3.3 Graphical Results
Comparison of potential distribution between homogeneous and layered soil.
Step Voltage Analysis

4. Rod Electrode in Homogeneous Soil
4.1 Data & Properties
Model of a vertical rod electrode embedded in uniform soil.

4.2 Post‑Processing
Computation of grounding resistance and field distribution.

4.3 Graphical Results
Electric field and potential maps around the rod.

5. Rod Electrode Emerging in Homogeneous Soil with Air Interface
5.1 Data & Properties
Definition of geometry including air–soil interface.

5.2 FEM Model
Mesh generation and boundary conditions for mixed‑medium simulation.

5.3 Post‑Processing
Analysis of electric field distortion due to the air boundary.

5.4 Graphical Results
Potential distribution and field intensity.
Step Voltage Analysis

Summary
This project provides a complete FEM‑based analysis of grounding electrodes in different soil conditions. It evaluates potential distribution, electric field behavior, and step voltage, offering insight into how geometry and soil stratification affect grounding performance.
